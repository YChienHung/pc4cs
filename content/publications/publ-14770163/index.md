publication: '*Neurocomputing*'
doi: 10.1016/j.neucom.2016.05.092
abstract: A number of Cellular Automata (CA)-based edge detectors have been developed
  recently due to the simplicity of the model and the potential for simultaneous removal
  of different types of noise in the process of detection. This paper introduced a
  novel edge detector using Outer Totalistic Cellular Automata. Its performance has
  been compared with other recently developed CA-based edge detectors, in addition
  to some classic methods, through testing images from a public library. Visual and
  quantitative measurement of similarity with manually marked correct edges confirmed
  the superiority of the proposed method over conventional and state-of-the-art CA-based
  edge detectors

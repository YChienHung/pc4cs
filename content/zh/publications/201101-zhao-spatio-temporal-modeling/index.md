abstract: The wavefront profile and the propagation velocity of waves in an 
  experimentally observedBelousov-Zhabotinskii reaction are analyzed and a 
  revised FitzHumgh-Nagumo (FHN) modelof these systems is identified. The ratio 
  between the excitation period and the recovery period,for a solitary wave is 
  studied, and included within the model. Averaged traveling velocities 
  atdifferent spatial positions are shown to be consistent under the same 
  experimental conditions.The relationship between the propagation velocity and 
  the curvature of the wavefront are alsostudied to deduce the diffusion 
  coefficient in the model, which is a function of the curvature ofthe wavefront
  and not a constant. The application of the identified model is demonstrated 
  onreal experimental data and validated using multistep ahead predictions.

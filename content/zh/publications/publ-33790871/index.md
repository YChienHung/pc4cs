abstract: Engineering systems are growing in complexity, requiring increasingly intelligent
  and flexible methods to account for and predict uncertainties in service. This paper
  presents a framework for dynamic uncertainty prediction under limited data (UPLD).
  Spatial geometry is incorporated with LSTM networks to enable real-time multistep
  prediction of quantitative and qualitative uncertainty over time. Validation is
  achieved through two case studies. Results demonstrate robust prediction of trends
  in limited and dynamic uncertainty data with parallel determination of geometric
  symmetry at each time unit. Future work is recommended to explore alternative network
  architectures suited to limited data scenarios.

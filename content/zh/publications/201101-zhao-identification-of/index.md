abstract: This Letter introduces a method for identifying geometrical models of 
  interface evolution, directly from experimental imaging data. These local 
  growth models relate normal growth velocity to curvature and its derivatives 
  estimated along the growing interface. Such models can reproduce many 
  qualitative featuresof dendritic crystal growth as well as predict 
  quantitatively its early stages of evolution. Numerical simulations and 
  experimental crystal growth data are used to demonstrate the applicability of 
  this approach

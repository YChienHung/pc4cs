abstract: '  To achieve greener and safer aeronautical operations, this paper considers
  the problem of reconstructing the three-dimensional (3D) geometric structure of
  aeronautical components. A novel framework that recovers the 3D shapes by means
  of convolutional neural network (ConvNets) and multiple view geometry (MVG) operating
  on Mask-R-CNN-segmented two-dimensional images is proposed. To achieve more accurate
  3D aircraft’s surface and exclude the invalid background structures, this paper
  innovatively integrates the environmental robustness of ConvNets and geometric adaptation
  of Mask-R-CNN into the MVG theory. The preliminary experiments show that the proposed
  framework is visual-comfortable, and it also accurately reconstructs the regions
  with damage to catch up with the inspection purpose.'

abstract: A nonlinear spatio-temporal system identi cation and analysis approach
  is used to study the dy-namical behaviour of the Belousov-Zhabotinsky (BZ) 
  chemical reaction process. In our previousstudy ([Guo et al., 2010]), the 
  dynamical behaviour of the BZ reaction in the spatial-temporaldomain has been 
  analysed by identifying a coupled map lattice (CML) model of the process 
  di-rectly from experimental data from a real BZ reaction experiment. In this 
  paper, the frequencydomain analysis of the dynamics near equilibrium is 
  carried out by mapping the obtained CMLmodel into higher order spatial 
  frequency response functions to reveal the nonlinear couplingand modulation 
  between the various initial spectral components in the process. As far as we 
  areaware this is the rst study of any real spatio-temporal system using a 
  spatio-temporal domainidenti cation and frequency domain analysis approach.

abstract: The identification problem for excitable media is investigated in this paper.
  A new scalar coupledmap lattice (SCML) model is introduced and the orthogonal least
  squares algorithm is employedto determinate the structure of the SCML model and
  to estimate the associated parameters. Asimulated pattern and a pattern observed
  directly from a real Belousov-Zhabotinsky reactionare identified. The identified
  SCML models are shown to possess almost the same local dynamicsas the original systems
  and are able to provide good long term predictions.
